## D1 — Check-in default: shared or private?

When a child does the daily check-in, what should the default be?

- **Shared** — Parent sees the check-in by default; child can switch any entry to "Just for me" (private). *Counselor's case: you see struggles early.*
- **Private** — Check-in defaults to "Just for me"; child opts in to share. *Psychologist's case: kids answer honestly when they know parents aren't reading.*

Either way, both channels exist — this is only about the default.

**Recommendation: Private.** Kids write the truth when it's theirs first. Honest check-ins feed better guidance, and the parent still sees the rhythm ("checked in 4 of 5 days").

Reply: `D1: shared` or `D1: private`

## D2 — Crystal chat for ages 13–17: unfiltered, or a safety floor?

You locked Crystal as unfiltered for the pilot. The expert panel's one genuine safety flag: for 13–17, unfiltered-with-no-floor is a real risk in four narrow cases — self-harm talk, eating-disorder talk, sexual content involving minors, abuse disclosures.

- **Unfiltered** — Keep the lock for teens too. No safety layer.
- **Safety floor** — Add a narrow duty-of-care layer: in those four cases only, Crystal routes to 988 / trusted-adult guidance. Framed as crisis care, NOT a filter. Normal conversation stays completely unfiltered and intelligent.

**Recommendation: Safety floor.** This isn't censorship — it's the 988 number in the four moments where a kid's life could be on the line. Everything else stays exactly as unfiltered as you locked it.

Reply: `D2: unfiltered` or `D2: safety`

## D3 — 13–15 check-ins: full text, or summary?

For 13–15 (the shared-agency tier), what does the parent see from check-ins?

- **Full** — Parent reads the full check-in text.
- **Summary** — Parent sees rhythm + themes ("checked in 4/5 days, flagged Geometry this week"), not the word-for-word text. Full text unlocks at 16.

**Recommendation: Summary.** Matches the tier (shared agency, not full parent visibility), and it keeps the teen honest knowing their private words stay private.

Reply: `D3: full` or `D3: summary`
